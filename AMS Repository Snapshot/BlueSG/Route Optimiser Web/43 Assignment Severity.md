# V2 assignment severity

Inputs: [[25 Work Styles and Area Lead]], [[26 End Requirements and Availability]], [[41 Travel Matrix Construction]], and [[55 Seven Zone Adjacency]].

Ordered categories:

1. Preferred;
2. Acceptable;
3. Disliked;
4. Long-Distance Cross-Zone;
5. Operationally Extreme.

Global thresholds: long repositioning 35 minutes, cross-zone 45 minutes, extreme 75 minutes. Style-specific local/adjacent thresholds refine the category.

End-progress can escalate severity near a deadline or soften a disliked move toward the required destination.

## Edges

Severity contributes to [[48 Rider Burden and Fairness]], [[47 Lexicographic Objective]], and [[49 V2 Status and Explanations]].

## Distinction

Severity is soft plan quality. Rejection belongs to [[44 Hard Feasibility]].
